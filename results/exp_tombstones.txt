Watch the capacity column rather than the size column. A table whose element
count never changes has no business growing, and the version that does is paying
for erases it performed hundreds of thousands of operations earlier.

Unlike every other experiment here, a batch cannot be repeated and a minimum
taken: each batch advances the table's state, so the timing column is single-shot
and correspondingly noisy. It is the capacity and footprint columns that carry
this result; the time follows them once the larger table stops fitting in cache.
